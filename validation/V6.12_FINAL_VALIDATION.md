## Accepted risks

1. **FINAL Repair removed** (`ACCEPTED_GENERALIZATION_RISK`): 0 Repair-complete successes observed in FINAL or fixed-N; ~46 s CPU on SJ97 for a discarded output. Repair remains active in PROBE.
2. **`LONG_RESCUE_SUCCESS_CLASS_EXISTS`**: the long Rescue success of fixed-N BK31 (~55747 nodes) is not preserved in FINAL with Rescue 3250. It stays intact in `Pallets_Objetivo`.
3. **PROBE Global internal drift**: Global in PROBE is still time-governed; its internal counters can vary (BK31 N=3 probe: 137474 vs 137004 generated states, same residual, same N and output).
4. **CP-SAT operational watchdog debt**: the deterministic limit is the primary cut; `max_time_in_seconds` is only an operational watchdog (`OPERATIONAL_TIMEOUT` is logged).
5. **FINAL Global 9500 calibrated from BK52/BK53**: BK52 succeeds at 4388 expansions (last depth starts at 4260) and BK53 at 6434 (last depth at 6306); 9500 is a 1.48x margin over the worst case, based on two real cases.
6. **BK43 intentional golden rebaseline** (`INTENTIONAL_GOLDEN_REBASELINE`): COMP changed from `885d929bff4dbc66` / `9a6a4652d41ab5e8` (pre-D5, `PRE_D5_REFERENCE_ONLY`) to `7764da5e6842a9b9`, caused by commit `acae72e` (D5, deterministic DFS/LNS work budgets). Post-D5 both `paralelo_lns` modes give the same value.
