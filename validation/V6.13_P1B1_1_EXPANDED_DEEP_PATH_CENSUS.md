## 5. Why one node budget cannot copy a CPU deadline

CPU per unit of work differs by case, so "90 s of CPU" is a different amount of work in each CD (classic, P1-A off):

| case | stage | ms / node | ≙ nodes in 90 s |
|---|---|---|---|
| BK31 | Rescue | 1.2–1.4 | ≈ 63 000–73 000 |
| BK52 | Rescue | 3.4–4.5 | ≈ 23 300 (measured) |
| BK52 | Repair | 5.2–5.4 | ≈ 18 200 (measured) |
| BK53 | Rescue / Repair | 7.2–8.2 | ≈ 11 000–12 500 |

A work budget calibrated as "the maximum observed" on one case (BK52 → 23 298) is the *shortest* of these equivalences and truncates the case that needs the most work (BK31 needs 55 649). Conversely, a budget large enough for BK31 is 2.4× BK52's RC2 window.
Also note the RC2 fragility this exposes: BK31 uses 57.5 s of its 90 s Rescue deadline, i.e. RC2 itself would flip BK31 from 98 to 94 on a machine ≥ 1.57× slower.

## 6. Is a single global budget possible?

Criterion A — **contract / output equivalence** (final COMP and LAYOUT = the Golden baseline, which is what the gate checks):

* BK31 requires Rescue `B ≥ 55 649` (strict `>` semantics) and has no upper bound.
* BK52 and BK53 are invariant to every B tested (23 297 … 80 000 and natural).
* ⇒ any Rescue budget in `[55 649, ≥ 80 000]` reproduces RC2 on all three deep-path cases simultaneously. **`SINGLE_GLOBAL_BUDGET_FEASIBLE` (output level).**
* Repair and Exact budgets were irrelevant to the output in every tested case; the only constraint on them is cost (Repair at 80 000 visits costs 261 s CPU in BK52 for no change; Repair has no natural end there).

Criterion B — **trajectory / event-signature equivalence** (same missing counts and work counts after each stage as RC2):

* BK52 RC2 = (Rescue 23 298, Repair 18 163, Exact 20/21). BK31 RC2 = Rescue to 55 649+.
* No single Rescue budget does both ⇒ **`SINGLE_GLOBAL_BUDGET_REFUTED` at trajectory level.** B2's contract ("same incumbent trajectory as B1") is unsatisfiable together with the Golden BK31, which is why it failed.

Caveats (they matter more than the verdict):

1. Evidence base is **3 deep-path cases**; the lower bound 55 649 is determined by **one** case (BK31). It is a floor for the known population, not a proof for future CDs.
2. BK52/BK53 are invariant *because* Global completes them. A CD where Global does not complete (as it does not for BK31 under truncation) would be sensitive to the upstream budgets again.
3. Rescue has a finite natural end in 3/3 cases (535 / 55 677 / 69 366) — a budget is a safety bound there, not the governor. Repair has no natural end in BK52 (≥ 400 077), so it is the one stage that genuinely needs a bound.
4. Exact: only BK52 depends on a bound (21 vs natural 33); no output depends on it.

## 7. Candidate variables for an adaptive budget (not implemented, not formulated)

Features at Rescue entry (counts only):

| case | SKUs | candidate towers | missing boxes / SKUs | placed boxes | ms/node | natural Rescue A |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| BK53 | 42 | 132 | 1 / 1 | 207 | 7.2–8.2 | 535 |

| BK52 | 32 | 108 | 25 / 12 | 89 | 3.4–4.5 | 69 366 |

* `A` rises with the **amount of missing demand** (1 → 11 → 25 boxes; 535 → 55 700 → 69 366) — monotone but n = 3.
* `ms/node` rises with **candidate-tower count** (52 → 108 → 132; 1.3 → 4 → 7.5) — monotone but n = 3 and includes stage-overhead effects on the tiny BK53 stage.
* Not explained by SKU count or placed boxes (BK53 has the most SKUs and cheapest `A`).
* These are hypotheses for a CPU-calibrated work unit (nodes × candidates?) or a demand-scaled cap; with three data points they cannot be validated. Needs a larger deep-path population.

## 8. Recommendation for a revised B2 (for decision, not implemented)

1. **Drop "budget = max observed on BK52".** It is inconsistent with the Golden BK31. Calibrate each stage from its *natural completion* (A) where one exists, never from a deadline-bound value (B).
2. **Rescue**: it terminates by itself in all known cases (max 69 366). Make the governor natural exhaustion + a high work safety cap above `max(A)` with margin (floor `55 649`, observed `69 366`), with the CPU deadline only as watchdog. Selecting the value is left open as requested.
3. **Repair**: the one stage without natural end; no output depends on it in the known set. Needs a cost-driven bound; keep it modest (evidence: 1 037 … 80 000 all give the same final output on BK52). Pick after a larger census.
4. **Exact**: cap ≥ natural max (33) or the existing 21; no output depends on it. Do not infer "21 is globally correct" from BK52.
5. **Global**: keep the deadline as watchdog; BK52/BK53 end `completo` ≈ 97 s ≪ 180 s.
6. **Accept that BK52's intermediate trajectory changes** (e.g. 25→3 instead of 25→14 after Rescue); the Golden output does not. The event contract of B2 ("same incumbent trajectory") should be replaced by "same final COMP/LAYOUT/attributes + stage classes".
7. **Before any implementation**: widen the deep-path population (the stress cases must come from known datasets or be approved explicitly), and keep BK31 in every B-gate. Re-running Golden 6 + the four BK31 tests is the minimum gate; it would have caught B2 immediately.
8. P1-A stays off inside migrated stages until B4; note that with P1-A on the same work is ≈ 2.3× cheaper in CPU, which changes nothing in the work counts.

## 9. Method, reproducibility, limits

* Harness: scratchpad `b11_census.py` (derived from the B1 harness): same stage instrumentation (check log, node counters), plus a *shadow* mode that injects `presupuesto_rescue_nodos` / `presupuesto_repair_nodos` kwargs and an Exact `max_intentos` cap (shared across pallets, deadline lifted) **only on the FIXED_N deep call**. Global is never altered. No file in `src/` or `tests/` was modified.
* RC2-faithful runs on a quiet machine are bit-identical across runs (BK52 ×5, BK31 ×3). Shadow runs are work-governed and therefore independent of load; Global in shadow runs ended `completo` / `estancado` before its deadline in all cases.
* `paralelo_lns=False` (pilot/pipeline mode) in all runs; BK31's golden is defined with `paralelo_lns=True` and gave the same hashes in the Golden-6 run under the B2 tree.
* The Mac was on battery for part of the session; this affects wall time, not CPU/work counts.
* Not done (out of scope): OPEN path (SJ87/SJ97 probes, B3), Piloto 2, C++, new lower bound, any solver change.
