# V6.13-R5 — Remaining CPU attribution (BK52, BK53)

Diagnostic only. No production code, budget, Repair, Exact, Global or OPEN path was changed. Base: `77022ad` (R4 closed). Everything below was measured with a scratchpad harness that wraps functions from outside (not committed); AC power the whole time (`power.log` empty). Numbers are CPU seconds (`process_time`) of `armar_pallets_bloques`; the dataset load (≈ 25 s CPU, 24.4–25.3 in every run) is outside it and reported separately.

## 0. Method and one measurement caveat

| tier | what is wrapped | use |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | only stage boundaries (exact-N, `_empacar_n_pallets`, Rescue, Repair, Exact, Global) | authoritative stage totals |
| 4 | tier 0 + `_lns_mejorar_pallets` + `_empacar_n_pallets_greedy` (1–2 calls) | authoritative fallback LNS vs DFS split (BK52) |
| 1 | tier 0 + DFS, snapshot/restore, reserve, greedy, Exact internals, validators | per-function split (×3 BK52/BK53) |
| 2 | tier 1 + `_candidatos_lote`, `_mejor_cuboide_para_sku`, `_podar_libres`, `_actualizar_libres_maxrects`, Global `_expandir_estado/_score_estado/_evaluar_viabilidad` | hot spots (×1 each) |

**Caveat (BK52 only).** Wrapping `_resolver_pallets_backtracking` inflates the *no-reserve fallback* DFS of BK52 from 20.4 s to 48.6 s (tier 1/3 total 394–399 s vs 366.6–368.8 s in tiers 0/4 and 367.7–371.5 s in the R4 gates). Node counts (30 001 DFS nodes, 46 LNS iterations), COMP/LAYOUT/attrs are identical in every tier, so it is a CPU cost of the instrument, not a behaviour change; I did not isolate its mechanism. BK53 is unaffected (tier 0 275.4 vs tier 1 276.1–280.0). Therefore: **stage totals come from tiers 0/4; tier-1/2 numbers are used for splits inside a stage, and BK52 rows in the fallback (and hot spots measured there) are upper bounds.** Every instrumented run reproduced the Golden COMP/LAYOUT/attrs (BK52 `e028452d4bf44586`/`0c931402980db937`/`2330d008f3d6553d`, BK53 `f172759840c3a5e7`/`eba170225b45cfc4`/`7dfe61240e231ea1`).

## 5. Repair risk classification

Evidence (all hashes identical to Golden unless stated):

| case | Repair budget | Repair CPU / rounds | missing in → out | Exact attempts (neighbourhood sizes `posiciones`) | Global | final |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| BK52 | 90 s (default) ×3 | 90.0 / 42 | 3 → 3 | 8 (90,100,26,133,52,150,148,·) | `completo` 4 388 | 114, Golden |
| BK52 | 30 s | 30.1 / — | 3 → 3 | 8, same sizes | `completo` | 114, Golden |
| BK52 | 5 s | 5.3 / 3 | 3 → 3 | 8, same sizes and same CP stats | `completo` | 114, Golden |
| BK53 | 1.6 s (natural) ×3 | 1.6 / 1 | 1 → 1 | 8 | `completo` 6 434 | 208, Golden |
| BK53 | 0.2 s | 0.3 / — | 1 → 1 | 8 | `completo` | 208, Golden |
| BK31 stress (Rescue cut at 23 297) — `SHADOW_STRESS_ONLY` | 90 s / 30 / 1 / 0.01 | 90.7 / 31.7 / 1.8 / 0.06 | 5 → 5 | 5 attempts in each run | does not complete (4 → 15) | 94 boxes, one hash in all four |

* **(a) Can a deadline variation change only the incumbent reaching Exact/Global?** Not observed: Repair's `mejor_mejora` is 0 in all 17 instrumented runs, missing counts do not move, and Exact receives identical neighbourhoods (same `posiciones`) at 5, 30 and 90 s. I did not hash the pallets leaving Repair, so "identical incumbent" is established at count/neighbourhood level, not at layout-hash level.
* **(b) Can it prevent Global from running or close the CD before Global?** No. Repair's deadline is local to Repair; the deep-path Global has no deadline (natural termination, watchdog 1800 s CPU) and always starts after Repair/Exact. In the runs Global ran and finished `completo` for BK52/BK53 at every Repair budget.
* **(c) Can it change the final solution (COMP/LAYOUT)?** Not observed: invariant for BK52 (5/30/90 s), BK53 (0.2/1.6 s), and also in the BK31 stress state where Global cannot complete (0.01–90 s, 94 boxes, same hashes).

**Classification: `PERFORMANCE_DEBT_ONLY`** for the known population. Residual (stated, not hidden): only 3 CDs have this path, and Repair has never produced an improvement in any run, so a case where a deadline-truncated Repair would have rescued a box *and* Global would not complete has never been observed — that scenario is unobserved, not disproved. Repair is also the stage where speed-ups do **not** save CPU: BK52's Repair runs to its 90 s deadline, so a faster Repair would do more work in the same 90 s (and change its trajectory) rather than finish earlier.

* **(a)** Yes in principle (fewer attempts run under a smaller deadline: 8 → 6 → 5 → 1), but the incumbent leaving Exact did not change in any run because no attempt after the first has ever improved.

## 8. Recommendation

* **Touch first (pure speed, work-invariant): items 1–3.** They attack ≈ 55–65 % of CPU in both CDs and can be gated with the same discipline as R2/R4 (identical work counters, expansion/state counts, COMP/LAYOUT/attrs, Golden 6, no budget change). Suggested order: `_podar_libres` + `_candidatos_lote` (largest, appear in every DFS-family stage), then Global score/viability, then Exact candidate generation.
* **Leave as registered debt, do not change now: Repair and Exact deadlines.** Both are `PERFORMANCE_DEBT_ONLY` on the evidence (final invariant to 5–90 s Repair, 0.05–30 s Exact; no improvement ever produced by Repair; Exact improved only once, deterministically, in the stress state). Their only observable cost is CPU (BK52 Repair 90 s). Do not optimise Repair internals before it has a natural/work-based end, otherwise the speed-up becomes more search, not less time.
* **Do not remove the no-reserve fallback or Repair on this evidence alone**; each needs a separate equivalence study (BK31 shows the final can depend on upstream when Global does not complete).
* The BK53 front-end (fallback LNS) is the single biggest block (120 s, 44 %); it is node/iteration-capped, so it is a speed target, not a correctness one.
