# V6.13-P1B2-R1 — Rescue natural exhaustion (FIXED_N deep path)

Code commit: `c76b492` ("feat: make fixed-N deep Rescue terminate naturally"), on top of `182f54c`.
Scope: **Rescue of the FIXED_N deep path only.** Repair, Exact, Global, the OPEN probes and the final materialization are untouched.

## 1. What changed

* `armar_pallets_bloques` (deep path, no `exact_n_incumbent`) calls `_empacar_n_pallets(..., gobernador_profundo=True)`.
* There, `rescue_solver(..., watchdog_cpu_segundos=1800.0)` runs with a `RelojTrabajoConWatchdog` (src/medidor_trabajo.py). The stage has **no work budget and no time budget**: it ends by its existing conditions — `solucion` (no critical SKU left) or `sin_progreso` (a full round without improvement). The 90 s CPU deadline passed by the caller is ignored in this mode.
* **Watchdog = safety only: 1800 s CPU** (5.4× the natural Rescue CPU of BK52, ≈ 335 s). If it fires: `stats["presupuesto"]["watchdog_disparado"]`, `metadata["fixed_n_deep_governor"]["watchdog_triggered"] = True`, `stop_reason = "watchdog"`, a WARNING is logged; the gates treat any trigger as FAIL. Nothing is returned silently as if it were equivalent.
* **P1-A is OFF inside the stage** (`RelojTrabajoConWatchdog.permite_p1a = False`; the gate in `_resolver_pallets_backtracking` reads it). Deferred pallet metrics and the `_area_union_xy` memo stay disabled, exactly as in RC2.
* No fixed node budget (the censuses showed one budget calibrated on BK52 — 23 298 — breaks BK31), no per-CD logic, no demand/SKU-size formulas.
* Not combined with the probe/final budgets: if any `presupuesto_*` kwarg is present the governor is not activated.
* Metadata (`fixed_n_deep_governor`): `{activo, modo: "rescue_agotamiento_natural", watchdog_cpu_segundos, watchdog_triggered, watchdog_stages, etapas: {rescue: {trabajo, unidad: "visitas", stop_reason, watchdog_triggered}}}`.

Repair, Exact and Global: their code, their kwargs and their CPU deadlines are identical to `a255751` (asserted by `tests/test_p1b2r1_rescue_natural_exhaustion.py`; the diff touches only `medidor_trabajo.py`, `rescue_solver.py` and the Rescue wiring + P1-A switch in `packing_bloques.py`).

Watchdog history: the first version used 600 s (1.8× the natural Rescue CPU of BK52). Under CPU throttling (§5) it fired and the stage went back to being governed by time. It was raised to 1800 s; nothing else was changed.

## 4. Known cost

R1 trades time for determinism: BK52 ≈ 617 s CPU (RC2 556 s). Rescue ≈ 335 s of that is the natural end that RC2 used to cut at 90 s; this is what R2 (P1-A inside Rescue) is meant to recover.

## 5. `ENVIRONMENT_INVALIDATED_RUNS` / `HARDWARE_THROTTLING_STRESS_EVIDENCE`

The first gate attempt switched the laptop to battery power mid-campaign (CPU heavily throttled / machine suspended; one run shows 36 395 s of wall for 1 139 s of CPU). Three runs were invalidated and are **not** used for hashes, performance or the gate; they are kept as technical evidence:

| run | CPU s | wall s | boxes | COMP | what happened |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| BK52 run 3 | 1 139 | 36 395 | 111 | `78e16a8153db8e2a` | the 600 s **Rescue watchdog fired** at 61 850 visits (natural end is 69 366) → stage cut by time; Global then hit its 180 s deadline (`timeout`, 30 932 states) |
| BK53 run 2 | 377 | 3 233 | 207 | `dab4ea0c72b785c3` | Rescue natural (535); **Global `timeout`** (58 360 states) → 207/208 |
| BK53 run 3 | 644 | 14 399 | 207 | `dab4ea0c72b785c3` | Rescue natural (535); **Global `timeout`** (53 449 states) → 207/208 |

They demonstrate two real things:

1. A 600 s watchdog is too narrow to be a pure safety barrier (hence 1800 s).
2. **FIXED_N Global still depends on CPU speed** and can change the result under throttling (BK53 207/208). Not touched by R1.
